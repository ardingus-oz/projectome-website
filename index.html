LAYER 03 / THE SCIENCE

Your genome is fixed.
Your epigenome is not.

DNA methylation is a set of chemical marks on your DNA that help control which genes are switched on or off. Unlike the DNA sequence itself, these marks can change over time, which is why they can be measured again and again over the course of treatment.

PLATFORM
Measured on the Illumina Infinium MethylationEPIC v2.0 array, which covers roughly 930,000 CpG sites.
SAMPLE

OUTPUTS

INTERPRETATION
